Diagram design toolkit

  • Sankey editor Flows between nodes, with widths on one fixed scale.
  • Ternary editor Trajectories of a mix of three shares over the years.
  • Treemap editor What totals are made up of, with every area on one scale.
  • Waterfall editor How totals change step by step, in one or two synced diagrams.
  • Bar chart editor Stacked or side by side, vertical or horizontal, with icons.
  • Chord editor Flows between nodes around a circle, for several scenarios.

Prompts for AI assistance

Each prompt teaches an AI chat assistant the YAML format of one editor. Show, download (as an .md file) or copy one to your clipboard, give it to the assistant with your (dummy) data or a description of the diagram, and open the YAML you get back as a .yaml file in the editor.

  • Sankey prompt
  • Ternary prompt
  • Treemap prompt
  • Waterfall prompt
  • Bar chart prompt
  • Chord prompt

Your data stays local: an editor reads the .yaml file in your browser and never sends it over the internet. Only what you share with an AI assistant leaves your computer.
